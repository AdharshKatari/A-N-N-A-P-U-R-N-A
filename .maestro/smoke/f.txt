
Here is the **step-by-step sequence to test all Annapurna APIs in Postman**, following the natural farmer and crop lifecycle:

---

### **Pre-requisites: Postman Environment Variables**
In Postman, set up an Environment with these variables:
* `base_url`: `http://localhost:8000` (or `http://10.0.2.2:8000` for Android emulator)
* `token`: *(Will be populated in Step 2)*
* `batch_id`: *(Will be populated in Step 4)*

---

### **Phase 1: Authentication & Onboarding**

#### **Step 1: Register Farmer Account**
* **Method & URL**: `POST {{base_url}}/api/v5/auth/register`
* **Headers**: `Content-Type: application/json`
* **Body (raw JSON)**:
  ```json
  {
    "username": "ramesh_farmer",
    "password": "FarmerPassword123!",
    "full_name": "Ramesh Kumar",
    "phone": "+919876543210",
    "district": "Nashik",
    "state": "Maharashtra"
  }
  ```

#### **Step 2: Login & Obtain JWT Token**
* **Method & URL**: `POST {{base_url}}/api/v5/auth/login`
* **Headers**: `Content-Type: application/json`
* **Body (raw JSON)**:
  ```json
  {
    "username": "ramesh_farmer",
    "password": "FarmerPassword123!"
  }
  ```
* **Postman Test Script** *(under the "Tests" tab to auto-save the token)*:
  ```javascript
  const res = pm.response.json();
  pm.environment.set("token", res.access_token);
  ```

> 💡 **For all subsequent requests**, add the Header:  
> `Authorization: Bearer {{token}}`

---

### **Phase 2: Metadata & Environmental Intelligence**

#### **Step 3: Get Supported Crops Taxonomy**
* **Method & URL**: `GET {{base_url}}/api/v5/metadata/crops`
* **Purpose**: Fetches the list of valid crop varieties (e.g., Tomato, Onion, Grapes) and base shelf-life parameters.

#### **Step 4: Fetch Live Weather by GPS Coordinates**
* **Method & URL**: `GET {{base_url}}/api/v5/weather?latitude=19.9975&longitude=73.7898`
* **Purpose**: Retrieves ambient temperature, humidity, and rainfall probability for Nashik farm coordinates.

#### **Step 5: Get Weather Spoilage & Heat Alerts**
* **Method & URL**: `GET {{base_url}}/api/v5/weather/alerts?latitude=19.9975&longitude=73.7898&crop_type=Tomato`
* **Purpose**: Evaluates Q10 thermal degradation and triggers alerts if high heat accelerates rot.

#### **Step 6: Query ISRO VEDAS Satellite Data**
* **Method & URL**: `GET {{base_url}}/api/v5/satellite/vedas/live?latitude=19.9975&longitude=73.7898`
* **Purpose**: Retrieves live soil moisture, NDVI vegetation health index, and Land Surface Temperature (LST).

---

### **Phase 3: Harvest & AI Quality Assessment**

#### **Step 7: AI Vision Assessment (Single Fruit / Sample)**
* **Method & URL**: `POST {{base_url}}/api/v5/vision/analyze`
* **Headers**: `Content-Type: multipart/form-data`
* **Body (form-data)**:
  * `file`: *(Select any crop image file, e.g. tomato.jpg)*
* **Response**: Returns AGMARK visual score (0-100), ripeness stage (*Unripe/Optimal/Overripe*), and fungal pathogen flag.

#### **Step 8: Create Harvest Batch**
* **Method & URL**: `POST {{base_url}}/api/v5/batches/harvest`
* **Headers**: `Content-Type: application/json`
* **Body (raw JSON)**:
  ```json
  {
    "crop_type": "Tomato",
    "variety": "Vaibhav Hybrid",
    "initial_weight_kg": 500,
    "latitude": 19.9975,
    "longitude": 73.7898,
    "field_name": "North Field Block A",
    "detected_ripeness_stage": "Optimal",
    "visual_quality_score": 88.5
  }
  ```
* **Postman Test Script**:
  ```javascript
  const res = pm.response.json();
  pm.environment.set("batch_id", res.batch_id || res.id);
  ```

---

### **Phase 4: Market Intelligence & Smart Move Optimization**

#### **Step 9: Fetch Live APMC Mandi Rates**
* **Method & URL**: `GET {{base_url}}/api/v5/mandi/prices?crop=Tomato`
* **Purpose**: Fetches real-time modal, min, and max prices across APMC Mandis (e.g., Lasalgaon, Nashik, Vashi Mumbai, Pune).

#### **Step 10: Simulate "Smart Move" What-If Scenarios**
* **Method & URL**: `POST {{base_url}}/api/v5/whatif/simulate`
* **Headers**: `Content-Type: application/json`
* **Body (raw JSON)**:
  ```json
  {
    "crop_type": "Tomato",
    "quantity_kg": 500,
    "visual_score": 88.5,
    "ambient_temp_c": 31.0,
    "latitude": 19.9975,
    "longitude": 73.7898,
    "ethylene_multiplier": 1.1
  }
  ```
* **Response**: Compares 4 strategies (*Sell Locally, Cold Storage, Processing/Bio-fuel, Transport to Mumbai*) with net profit, transport cost, and spoilage risk.

---

### **Phase 5: Batch Details, Cryptographic Passport & Traceability**

#### **Step 11: Get Individual Batch Details**
* **Method & URL**: `GET {{base_url}}/api/v5/batches/{{batch_id}}`
* **Purpose**: Retrieves all metadata, harvest timestamp, weight, and status for the active batch.

#### **Step 12: Generate Cryptographic Batch Passport (QR)**
* **Method & URL**: `GET {{base_url}}/api/v5/passport/{{batch_id}}`
* **Purpose**: Returns the cryptographically signed passport with SHA-256 seal, AGMARK certification, and QR verification token.

#### **Step 13: Verify Batch Passport Token (Buyer/Inspector Verification)**
* **Method & URL**: `POST {{base_url}}/api/v5/passport/verify?token=PASTE_TOKEN_HERE`
* **Purpose**: Validates QR authenticity and anti-tamper signature for market buyers.

#### **Step 14: Get Thermal Degree-Hour Analytics**
* **Method & URL**: `GET {{base_url}}/api/v5/logs/{{batch_id}}/analytics`
* **Purpose**: Computes cumulative heat units accumulated during transit and remaining shelf-life hours.

---

### **Phase 6: Multilingual Speech-to-Speech Assistant ("Annapurna Vaani")**

#### **Step 15: Query Multilingual Voice Assistant**
* **Method & URL**: `POST {{base_url}}/api/v5/voice/assistant`
* **Headers**: `Content-Type: application/json`
* **Body (raw JSON)**:
  ```json
  {
    "query": "టమాటల ధరలు ఎక్కడ బాగున్నాయి?",
    "language": "te",
    "crop": "Tomato",
    "location": "Nashik",
    "latitude": 19.9975,
    "longitude": 73.7898
  }
  ```
* **Response**:
  ```json
  {
    "spoken_text": "నాసిక్ మార్కెట్లో టమాట క్వింటాలుకు ₹2,800 పలుకుతోంది. వాషి మార్కెట్లో ₹3,400 ఉంది.",
    "english_translation": "Tomato is trading at ₹2,800/quintal in Nashik and ₹3,400 in Vashi.",
    "action": "NAVIGATE_MARKET",
    "action_label": "మార్కెట్ ధరలు చూడండి"
  }
  ```

#### **Step 16: Synthesize Regional Audio Stream**
* **Method & URL**: `GET {{base_url}}/api/v5/voice/synthesize?text=టమాటల+ధరలు+బాగున్నాయి&lang=te`
* **Purpose**: Streams high-fidelity regional Indian TTS audio MP3 for voice playback.

---

### **Phase 7: Dashboard & Notifications**

#### **Step 17: Get Farmer Dashboard Overview**
* **Method & URL**: `GET {{base_url}}/api/v5/dashboard`
* **Purpose**: Returns summary card numbers (active batches, total tonnage, crop varieties, recent activity feed).

#### **Step 18: Get Notifications & Alerts**
* **Method & URL**: `GET {{base_url}}/api/v5/notifications`
* **Purpose**: Lists unread agronomic alerts, weather warnings, and Mandi price surge notifications.

---
