Its heartbeat repeatedly fails to write the `sessions` file with a Windows file-lock error. This reproduced with normal and isolated temporary Maestro homes, after clearing ADB forwards and restarting `dev.mobile.maestro`. This is the genuine automation blocker—not an app or PATH defect.
